# 🔗 Homaatri Action Dependencies & Logical Tool Build Sequence

This document details the **Cross-Agent Action Dependency Analysis** and establishes the exact **4-Tier Logical Build Order** for Homaatri's AI Agent Tools.

## 🏗️ Why Build Sequence Matters

Building AI Agent Tools in the wrong order causes development bottlenecks (e.g. building `Customer.view_chefs_sorted_by_distance` before `Chef.set_daily_batch_capacity` exists means testing customer discovery fails because no kitchen data exists!).

By analyzing data producers vs. data consumers, we group all tools into a **4-Tier Build Order**.

## 📊 Phase-by-Phase Action Dependency Breakdown

### Tier 1: Foundation (Chef Domain & Master Cutoff Engine)
* **Build Order**: **1st (Zero Dependencies)**
* **Why**: Chefs, menus, and daily batch capacities are the foundational data providers. Customers cannot browse menus, calculate distances, or place orders until Chef data exists.
* **Tools in Tier 1**:
  1. `Chef.get_chef_profile`
  2. `Chef.set_daily_batch_capacity`
  3. `Chef.toggle_dish_stock_status`
  4. `Chef.get_chef_inventory_status`
  5. `Master.validate_meal_window_cutoff_clock`

---

### Tier 2: Ingestion & Checkout (Customer Agent Tools)
* **Build Order**: **2nd (Depends on Tier 1)**
* **Why**: Customer tools rely on Tier 1 Chef menus and Master Cutoff Clock to validate order timing, compute Haversine distance, calculate subtotals, and generate payment link URLs.
* **Tools in Tier 2**:
  1. `Customer.get_customer_profile` & `Customer.register_customer_profile`
  2. `Customer.update_customer_location_from_whatsapp_pin`
  3. `Customer.view_chefs_sorted_by_distance` (Depends on `Chef.chef_profiles` & `Chef.set_daily_batch_capacity`)
  4. `Customer.view_chef_menu` (Depends on `Chef.chef_menu_items`)
  5. `Customer.create_customer_order` (Depends on `Master.validate_meal_window_cutoff_clock`)
  6. `Customer.add_item_to_order` (Depends on `Chef.chef_menu_items`)
  7. `Customer.generate_initial_payment_link` & `generate_topup_payment_link`
  8. `Master.process_payment_webhook_confirmation`

---

### Tier 3: Cutoff Orchestration & Cooking (Master Route Solver & Chef Packing)
* **Build Order**: **3rd (Depends on Tier 2 Confirmed Orders)**
* **Why**: At 12:00 PM (Lunch) & 7:00 PM (Dinner) cutoff, confirmed orders exist. Master Agent locks batch windows, calls GCP Route Optimization API (ONCE), and dispatches consolidated checklists to Chefs. Chefs prepare food and broadcast `PACKED_READY` signals.
* **Tools in Tier 3**:
  1. `Master.lock_meal_window_batch`
  2. `Master.orchestrate_gcp_route_optimization` (Depends on confirmed `Customer.customer_orders`)
  3. `Chef.get_chef_batch_checklist` & `Chef.get_batch_item_totals`
  4. `Chef.mark_order_packed_ready` (Triggers notification to Driver Agent)

---

### Tier 4: Fulfillment & Driver Dispatch (Driver Agent & Cross-Domain Relays)
* **Build Order**: **4th (Depends on Tier 3 Master Routes & Packed Food)**
* **Why**: Drivers read the master route sequence generated by Master Agent in Tier 3. Drivers navigate single-leg links (`Stop N ➔ Stop N+1`), complete apartment gate drop-offs, and report exceptions.
* **Tools in Tier 4**:
  1. `Driver.get_driver_profile` & `Driver.update_driver_location`
  2. `Driver.get_assigned_route_itinerary` (Depends on `Master.orchestrate_gcp_route_optimization`)
  3. `Driver.dispatch_next_leg_navigation_link` (Single-leg link dispatch)
  4. `Driver.mark_driver_reached_stop` & `Driver.mark_orders_picked_up`
  5. `Driver.mark_gate_delivery_completed` (Triggers `Master.relay_gate_delivery_completed_to_customer`)
  6. `Driver.report_unlocatable_address_hitl` & `Driver.report_vehicle_delay_alert`
  7. `Master.relay_customer_dietary_request_to_chef` & HITL Relays
