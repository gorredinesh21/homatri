Homaatri — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Homaatri ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Homaatri Home Meal Concierge platform servicing customers, home chefs, and delivery partners via WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

To facilitate meal discovery, order placement, and delivery route optimization, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Delivery Addresses & Location Data: Delivery addresses, apartment/locality details, and GPS coordinates provided for order delivery.
  • Order Preferences: Dietary preferences (Veg/Non-Veg), meal choices, and subscription schedules.
  • Conversation History: Transcripts of interactions with our automated WhatsApp concierges for order fulfillment and customer support.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Process and confirm daily meal orders with verified home kitchens.
  • Assign delivery routes to authorized delivery partners.
  • Provide real-time order status updates via WhatsApp notifications.
  • Provide customer support through our Admin Resolution Desk.

3. Data Protection & Security

Your data is stored securely in encrypted production databases on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate with official third-party APIs for core platform operation:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API: For messaging transmission under Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Razorpay: For secure payment gateway processing.
  • Google Cloud Platform / Vertex AI: For secure cloud hosting and AI conversational processing.

5. Data Rights & Opt-Out

You may request deletion of your account profile or opt out of automated messages at any time by messaging "STOP" or contacting our support team at admin@homatri.in.
